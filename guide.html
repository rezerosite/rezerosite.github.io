AL and Priscilla plot:

Priscilla’s Cheers for Me! Entertainment Part
The Day I Gave Up on Being a Following Star
Pride and Prejudice and Zombies
Priscilla’s Cheers for Me: Drunk Father Edition
Sunlight on the Water

My Fair Bad Lady (Natsumi’s first appearance)
A Love Song for EMT
Emily’s Maid Path
The First Mayonnaise Disturbance
Otto’s Bittersweet Peddling Trade Log (OTTO FUMBLES A BADDIE)
The Three Idiots Set Out! Earth Spider Episode
The Three Idiots Set Out! Cursed Goddess Statue Episode (Natsumi’s second appearance)
Poltergeist Story
(Tip: always check if a side story doesn’t have an "after" or extra)
 Elegant Lesson
Golden Siblings
Royal Selection: First Contact
First Mission (Adelheid and Julius) [!READ THE AFTER!]

Omega (Echidna) plot:

The Witch’s After Tea Party
The Witch’s After Tea Party: One Wild Night
Omega in Wonderland
Witches Trials

Anastasia camp:

Early side stories with Anastasia in the title if you are interested
Kararagi Girl Meets Cats
Lugunican Hustle
Anastasia’s Companion Searching Chronicle (they journey to Vollachia)

Felt camp:

Early side stories with Felt in the title if you are interested
Felt’s Royal Selection Life from Zero
Royal Selection Life From Zero: The Golden Lion and the Sword Saint
Riot in Flanders
Side stories corresponding to ex vol 2,3,6 contain Astrea family so related

Crush camp:

Ex novel 1, and if you liked it, read all side stories with "Duchess" in them

Arc 7 and 8 important ss:

The Dragon Kingdom: The Chronicle of the Search for the Missing Boy
The Dragon Kingdom: Meili’s Witchbeast Observation Record
Stand by Me Pleiades, all of them
The Land of Wolves: Death unto the Weak, No Mercy
Brotherhood of Pleidas

Arc 9 reads:

The Land of Nascent Wolves: Undead Busters
Kararagi Reaper Incident
No Stella, No Life
Please just read everything that comes out after this (So most of the stuff that appears after volume 43 on eminent site); the author became unpredictable
